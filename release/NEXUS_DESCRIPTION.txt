[size=5][b]A Bit Better Traversal Movement[/b][/size]
[i]For Final Fantasy VII Rebirth (PC, game version 1.0.0.5; 1.0.0.4 in beta)[/i]

Moving around the world in Rebirth can feel locked in and stiff: every ledge plays a full animation before you get control back, you can't steer a drop, rolls stop dead at small edges, and Cloud happily runs in place against walls. This mod smooths all of that out without changing how the game looks, and every part of it can be tuned or turned off while you play.

[size=4][b]Main features[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Faster traversal[/b] - vaults, climbs, jump-downs and cliff stops play faster (separately tunable), so you get control back sooner. The small automatic hop over steps keeps its normal speed so it never looks frozen.
[*][b]Faster climbing sections (new in 1.1)[/b] - climbing walls, ladders, ropes, poles, zip lines, the grappling hook and narrow gaps play faster, with much shorter pauses between handholds.
[*][b]Control back at the end of a traversal (new in 1.3)[/b] - no more waiting for every landing or vault animation to finish: press dodge / Acrobacias while landing to roll or start the next traversal right away, and end a vault or climb with the stick or the button once your feet are on the floor.
[*][b]Ledges to the side count (new in 1.2)[/b] - no need to face a ledge dead-on: with nothing straight ahead, ledges up to 35 degrees to either side also offer the prompt and the button traversal.
[*][b]Snappier running (new in 1.1)[/b] - starting to run, stopping and turning around play faster, so the character reacts sooner to the stick. The running animation itself is unchanged.
[*][b]Steer while falling[/b] - turn toward where you push the stick (or WASD) during drops and for a moment after landing, like normal walking.
[*][b]Land where you fall[/b] - no more snapping onto the game's pre-planned landing spot. Where a jump has to reach a specific platform on another level, the game's placement is kept.
[*][b]Landing momentum[/b] - keep part of your speed after landing instead of stopping on the spot.
[*][b]Longer dodge roll[/b] outside combat, closer to the combat one.
[*][b]Rolls don't turn into climbs[/b] - rolling at a wall no longer vaults you up it mid-roll. Standing next to a ledge, the button still climbs as usual.
[*][b]Roll off small ledges[/b] - a roll carries you over small cliffs and edges with its own speed instead of stopping at the edge. Big drops and out-of-bounds edges still stop you.
[*][b]Breathing room between traversals[/b] - a short pause after a big traversal, so two ledges close together don't cut each other's animations short.
[*][b]Acrobacias prompt closer to the edge[/b] - the prompt (and the button traversal) only kicks in when you're actually near the ledge. Fully adjustable.
[*][b]No running in place against walls[/b] - push into a wall and the character stops instead of running on the spot. Low obstacles you can hop, small props and other characters don't count.
[*][b]Hands on the wall[/b] - against a tall wall, the character turns to face it and rests both hands on it. If that wall can be climbed, the Acrobacias prompt shows from there at the game's normal distance.
[*][b]Camera follows the fall[/b] - when you roll or drop off a ledge, the camera now follows you down instead of hanging back and catching up only after you land.
[*][b]Everything live-tunable[/b] - one commented ini file, changes apply within a second while the game runs (the [i][camera][/i] options need a game restart).
[/list]

[size=4][b]What's new in 1.3.1[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Roll off more ledges[/b] - deeper drops are allowed when the ground below is part of the game's own walkable area (lower floors, broken floors, the level below a platform), up to 25 m. Out-of-bounds ground isn't part of it, so those edges still stop you ([i]max_drop_walkable[/i]).
[*][b]Drop prompt closer to the edge[/b] - jump-downs only offer the prompt when you're near the edge; climbs and vaults keep their distance ([i]drop_prompt_reach[/i]).
[*][b]Fixed[/b] - getting stuck against walls until letting go of the stick.
[*][b]Game version 1.0.0.4 (beta)[/b] - the same dwmapi.dll now also runs on 1.0.0.4. Not yet played on that version: reports and logs welcome.
[*][b]Updating:[/b] just replace dwmapi.dll; your ini keeps your values and gets the new keys on the first start.
[/list]

[size=4][b]What's new in 1.3[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Control comes back at the end of a traversal: roll or start the next traversal right away while landing, and end vaults and climbs with the stick or button once your feet are down ([i]landing_cancel[/i]).
[*]The short pause after a traversal no longer blocks the button - only automatic traversals wait.
[*]No accidental drops when running along an edge: ledges found to the side only offer the prompt.
[*]Pushing diagonally into a wall slides along it.
[*]Rolls from standing still go as far as running ones, and rolls keep their reach up slopes.
[*][b]Updating:[/b] just replace dwmapi.dll; your ini keeps your values and gets the new keys on the first start.
[/list]

[size=4][b]What's new in 1.2[/b][/size]

[*]A roll can still turn into a climb at its very start (button right next to a ledge), but not when it reaches a wall later ([i]roll_vault_start[/i]).
[*]Wall stop no longer stops you at party members or other characters.
[*]Optional shorter animation blends ([i][transitions][/i], off by default).
[/list]

[size=4][b]What's new in 1.1[/b][/size]
[list]

[size=4][b]Installation[/b][/size]
[list=1]
[*]Open the game folder: Steam > Final Fantasy VII Rebirth > right-click > Manage > Browse local files.
[*]Copy the [b]End[/b] folder from the archive into it, merging folders. You should end up with:
[code]<game folder>\End\Binaries\Win64\dwmapi.dll
<game folder>\End\Binaries\Win64\TraversalSpeed.ini[/code]
[*]Start the game. [b]TraversalSpeed.log[/b] appears next to the dll; its first lines confirm the mod loaded.
[/list]
[b]Uninstall:[/b] delete dwmapi.dll (and TraversalSpeed.ini / TraversalSpeed.log) from End\Binaries\Win64.

[b]Settings:[/b] edit [b]End\Binaries\Win64\TraversalSpeed.ini[/b] while the game runs. Every option is explained in the file; [i]enabled=0[/i] turns the whole mod off.

[size=4][b]Compatibility[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Game version 1.0.0.5 only.[/b] On any other version the mod detects it, says so in the log and stays inactive - it won't break anything.
[*]Loads through [b]dwmapi.dll[/b], so it conflicts with any other mod that also ships a dwmapi.dll. It works alongside mods that use other loader files.
[*]No game files are modified - remove the dll and the game is vanilla again.
[*]Linux / Steam Deck (Proton): untested. You will likely need the launch option [i]WINEDLLOVERRIDES="dwmapi=n,b" %command%[/i]
[/list]

[size=4][b]Disclaimer - where it might go wrong[/b][/size]
This mod changes how the character moves in a game full of hand-placed jumps, ledges and scripted spots, and it has been tested mostly with Cloud in the open world. Things I expect could still misbehave:
[list]
[*][b]Landing where you fall[/b] - in dungeons, platforming or scripted jumps that must land on one exact spot, some jumps may behave oddly. If one does, set [i]free_fall=0[/i] and please tell me where.
[*][b]Rolling off ledges[/b] - very rough or unusual edges may still stop a roll. Out-of-bounds protection: drops deeper than [i]max_drop[/i] only go when the ground below is on the game's walkable area. If you find an edge that lets you somewhere you shouldn't be, tell me where (or set [i]max_drop_walkable=0[/i]).
[*][b]Wall stop[/b] - may occasionally miss a wall, or stop you against odd geometry.
[*][b]Hands on the wall[/b] - it reuses a door-push animation, so it can clip or look off on uneven walls ([i]wall_hand=0[/i] turns it off).
[*][b]Ending traversals early[/b] - it ends them the game's own way and only on the ground, but if a scripted jump or platforming section misbehaves, set [i]landing_cancel=0[/i] and tell me where.
[*][b]Faster run starts, stops and turns[/b] - if the feet look like they slide somewhere, lower [i]run_start[/i], [i]run_stop[/i] or [i]turn[/i].
[*][b]Shorter prompt reach[/b] also means standing closer to a ledge for the button to start a climb or drop.
[*]Other party members, story segments, chocobo and vehicle sections have seen less testing.
[/list]
